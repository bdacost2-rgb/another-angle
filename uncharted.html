    Nothing to ask yet

    When a route runs into fog, the Compass stops, shows what already exists nearby and what could exist there, and asks how you feel about it. It will appear here, under the map.

    How it learns (not if-then rules)
    1. Taste with uncertainty. A Bayesian logistic model spans the whole map. It keeps a best guess of what you like and how unsure it is everywhere. The fog you see is that uncertainty.
    2. Uncharted spots. It looks for places your taste rates highly (or might, if it's unsure) where few existing works sit. Those blank areas are the targets.
    3. Route search. A* plans a path from where you are now to each target. Walking through areas you dislike costs more, so two people get different routes.
    4. Checkpoints only when they matter. It stops at the foggiest point on the route, where your answer teaches it the most (active learning). Love, closer, no and weirder each update the model, and the routes replan live.
    5. Arrival. When the fog along the route clears, it has found a place and time that never existed, built from your blend of themes. That becomes your assignment, for a photo or for writing.

    Everything here runs in your browser. The map is built from CLIP embeddings of every photo and passage in the archive, and it starts from the picks and hearts you made in Another Angle. Your votes here train the same taste model, so your feed gets better too.
